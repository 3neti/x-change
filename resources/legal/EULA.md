## 2. What the Service does

The Service provides software for creating and processing instructions, Pay Codes, collections, disbursements, settlement workflows, and related records. It may transmit instructions to banks, electronic-money issuers, payment networks, insurers, messaging providers, and other independent service providers.

The Service is not presented as a bank account, deposit account, or consumer digital wallet. A displayed Client Funds or Treasury Position balance is a system record of attributed funds and instruction status; it is not itself money stored inside the application.

The Service's ledger is intended to preserve attribution to each Principal, including available, reserved, instructed, executed, returned, or otherwise reconciled amounts. You acknowledge this provider-held pooled-account arrangement and authorize the Service to maintain principal-specific ledger attribution and to submit permitted instructions under the applicable Treasury Mandate.

Client Funds must not be treated as the Service operator's operating revenue. Fees and commercial charges must be identified separately from principal and pass-through amounts.
